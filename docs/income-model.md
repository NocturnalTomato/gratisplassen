# Income Model — Gratis Plassen (gratisplassen.vercel.app)

## What the website is

Gratis Plassen is a free, non-commercial public service website that helps
people (with a focus on women) find nearby public and semi-public toilets in
the Netherlands — including which ones are free, which are paid, and what
facilities they offer (toilet paper, hand-washing, sanitary product
dispensers, showers), based on crowdsourced reviews.

## How the website makes money

The website does **not sell any goods, services, subscriptions, or digital
products**. There is no paywall, no e-commerce checkout, and no recurring
billing tied to product delivery.

The only source of income is **voluntary, one-time donations** from visitors
who want to support the running costs of the site (hosting, domain, database).
This works the same way as a "Buy Me a Coffee" or Ko-fi tip button:

- A small button ("☕ Trakteer me op koffie" — "Buy me a coffee") is shown on
  every page of the site.
- Clicking it takes the visitor to a Stripe-hosted payment page (Stripe
  Payment Link / Checkout) where they can voluntarily contribute an amount of
  their own choosing (e.g. a suggested €3 / €5 / €10).
- No product, service, membership, or content access is granted in exchange
  for the donation — it is a gift to support the maintainer, not a purchase.
- Donations are one-time by default; there is no subscription product behind
  this button.

## Who operates it

The site is run by a single individual as a small, non-commercial hobby
project (not a registered company). Revenue, if any, is limited to the
voluntary donations described above and is used to cover hosting/domain
costs.

## Expected volume

This is a small, early-stage project. Donation volume is expected to be low
and irregular (occasional one-time tips), not a predictable recurring
revenue stream.
